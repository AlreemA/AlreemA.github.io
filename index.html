Hello, I'm
Alreem.

My name is Alreem Alkaabi, and I am currently in my final year at United Arab Emirates University (UAEU), pursuing a Bachelor's degree in Computer Science with a minor in Mathematics.

Four years ago, I chose to major in Computer Science because I was fascinated by artificial intelligence. At the time, AI was rapidly gaining attention and seemed almost like magic to me. The idea that machines could generate text, solve problems, and learn from data sparked my curiosity, and I wanted to understand how it all worked. That curiosity eventually grew into a passion for technology, problem-solving, and the potential of AI to create meaningful impact.

As graduation approaches, I often reflect on the goals I set for myself when I first began university. I created a list of achievements, experiences, and milestones that I wanted to accomplish before graduating, and I am now focused on making the most of my remaining time as a student and ensuring that I achieve as many of those goals as possible.
